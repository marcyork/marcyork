01 / Research

Questions at the intersection of humans and machines.

My cross-disciplinary research focuses on digital culture, media art, digital signatures, perception and knowledge systems. It combines discourse analysis and systems-oriented approaches with affect theory, visual studies, feminist epistemology, post-structural thought and the practical methods of artistic research.

Across these fields, I ask how computational systems reorganise what can be seen, known, signed, shared and remembered—and how these changes affect subjectivity and political agency.

05 / Biography

Georg Eckmayr

Georg Eckmayr is a Vienna-based artist, researcher and lecturer. He studied digital art under Peter Weibel at the University of Applied Arts Vienna, where he completed a doctoral degree in media studies in 2016. His work spans moving images, installations and coded structures, bringing artistic practice into dialogue with media theory and research into digital culture.
